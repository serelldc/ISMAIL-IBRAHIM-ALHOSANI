Criminal Defense Lawyer in Dubai

A criminal accusation in the UAE moves quickly — police referral to the Public Prosecution can happen within days, and what you say or fail to say at that stage often shapes the entire case. Early, informed representation changes outcomes.

UAE criminal procedure gives the accused the right to legal representation from the police interrogation stage onward, but that right is only useful if exercised — statements given without counsel present are frequently the evidence a case turns on.

Criminal cases in Dubai typically proceed through three stages: police investigation, referral to and investigation by the Public Prosecution, and — if charges are filed — trial before the Court of First Instance, with rights of appeal to the Court of Appeal and, in some cases, the Court of Cassation. Each stage has distinct procedural rights and deadlines.

The Public Prosecution in the UAE holds significant investigative authority, including the power to order detention pending investigation. Securing release, or at minimum limiting detention, often depends on how quickly a defense lawyer engages and what representations are made at the prosecutorial stage, before the matter ever reaches a courtroom.

We represent individuals accused of criminal offences at every stage — police interview, Public Prosecution investigation, trial, and appeal — working to protect procedural rights, challenge the evidence against the accused, and pursue the most favourable outcome available on the facts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I have a lawyer present during a police interview in the UAE?

Yes — the accused has the right to legal representation from the police stage onward, and exercising that right early can materially affect how the case develops.

What happens after the Public Prosecution investigates my case?

The prosecution can dismiss the case, refer it to trial, or in some cases pursue a settlement, depending on the offence and the evidence gathered during its investigation.

Can I be prevented from leaving the UAE during a criminal case?

Yes — a travel ban can be imposed while a criminal matter is pending, and lifting it typically requires a specific application addressing the circumstances of the case.

How long does a criminal case take from investigation to trial?

Timelines vary significantly depending on the offence and complexity, but straightforward matters can move from complaint to first hearing within a matter of weeks, while more complex cases take considerably longer.

Can a criminal conviction be appealed in Dubai?

Yes — convictions from the Court of First Instance can generally be appealed to the Court of Appeal, and in certain circumstances further to the Court of Cassation on points of law.
