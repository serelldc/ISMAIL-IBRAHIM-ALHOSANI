Brand Protection & Anti-Counterfeiting Lawyer in Dubai

Counterfeit goods bearing a brand's trademark can damage reputation and divert sales faster than any single enforcement action can address, which is why an ongoing brand protection strategy matters as much as reacting to individual incidents.

Brand protection combines proactive measures — customs recordal, market monitoring, and online platform enforcement — with reactive tools like raids and litigation when counterfeit activity is identified.

Customs recordal allows border authorities to intercept suspected counterfeit shipments before they enter the market, making it one of the more efficient tools available where counterfeiting is a significant concern.

Physical and online market monitoring helps identify counterfeit activity early, whether that's a market stall selling fake goods or a listing on an e-commerce platform using a brand's trademark without authorization.

We build brand protection programs combining customs recordal, monitoring, and enforcement coordination, and we act quickly when counterfeit activity is identified to limit ongoing harm.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does customs recordal actually do?

It allows border authorities to identify and intercept shipments suspected of carrying counterfeit goods bearing your registered trademark before they enter the market.

How can counterfeit listings on e-commerce platforms be removed?

Most major platforms have established enforcement mechanisms for trademark rights holders, and we handle these takedown requests as part of an ongoing monitoring program.

Is a one-time enforcement action enough to address counterfeiting?

Usually not — counterfeiting tends to recur through new channels once one source is stopped, which is why an ongoing brand protection program is generally more effective than isolated actions.

What happens during a coordinated raid against counterfeit goods?

We work with relevant authorities to identify the location, coordinate the timing and legal basis for the raid, and pursue seizure of the counterfeit goods found.

How do I know if my brand is at meaningful risk of counterfeiting?

This depends on factors like product category, market value, and geographic reach, and we can assess your specific brand's exposure to recommend an appropriately scaled protection program.
