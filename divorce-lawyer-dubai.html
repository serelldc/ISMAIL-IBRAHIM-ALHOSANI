Divorce Lawyer in Dubai

Divorce in the UAE is governed by personal status law, and for expatriate couples, by whichever law applies to their nationality or marriage — understanding which framework applies to your case shapes every decision that follows.

UAE courts generally apply Emirati personal status law by default, but foreign nationals may in some cases request their home country's law to apply to matters such as divorce grounds or financial settlement — an option that isn't automatic and depends on how the request is made and evidenced.

Divorce proceedings in Dubai can be contested or uncontested, and the distinction matters significantly for timeline, cost, and the degree of court involvement. Reaching agreement on custody and financial matters before filing often shortens the process considerably.

For expatriate couples, questions about which country's law governs the divorce, how a foreign marriage contract is treated, and how assets held abroad are addressed add layers of complexity beyond what UAE national couples typically face.

We represent clients through the full divorce process — from initial filing through family guidance committee proceedings, court hearings, and, where necessary, appeal — working to protect our clients' interests in custody, financial settlement, and property matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does UAE law automatically apply to my divorce if I'm not a UAE national?

Not automatically — foreign nationals may in some circumstances request application of their home country's law, but this depends on how the request is made and the specific facts of the case.

What's the difference between contested and uncontested divorce?

An uncontested divorce proceeds where both spouses agree on the key terms, generally making it faster and less costly, while a contested divorce involves disputed issues that require court determination.

Do I have to go through a reconciliation process before filing for divorce?

Many cases pass through a family guidance or reconciliation stage first, though the specific requirement depends on the circumstances of the case.

How is child custody typically decided in a UAE divorce?

Custody decisions consider the best interests of the child along with applicable legal principles, and outcomes vary based on the ages of the children and the specific circumstances of each parent.

Can a foreign divorce judgment be recognized in the UAE?

In many cases a foreign divorce judgment can be recognized and enforced in the UAE through the appropriate legal process, though this depends on the judgment and the jurisdiction it was issued in.
