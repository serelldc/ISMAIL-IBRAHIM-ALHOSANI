What Is an Interpol Red Notice, and How Can It Be Challenged in the UAE?

General information · Not legal advice

A Red Notice can surface suddenly at an airport or bank. Here is what it actually is, what it is not, and the legal routes available to challenge it.

An Interpol Red Notice is a request to law enforcement worldwide to locate and provisionally arrest a person pending extradition or similar legal action. It is issued by Interpol's General Secretariat at the request of a member country. It is not an international arrest warrant: each member country decides for itself whether and how to act on it.

In practice, the effects can be serious. A person named in a Red Notice may be detained when crossing a border or transiting an airport, may find bank accounts reviewed or frozen, and may be refused visas. Because the UAE is an Interpol member and a major transit hub, exposure can arise without any prior warning.

Interpol has its own rules, and its Constitution prohibits notices of a predominantly political, military, religious, or racial character. Notices that breach these rules can be challenged through Interpol's Commission for the Control of Files (CCF). Separately, if a country files a formal extradition request, the matter is reviewed in the UAE under its legal framework for international judicial cooperation, and defenses can be raised.

How a Red Notice Challenge Works

  1. Confirm Whether a Notice ExistsA lawyer can help establish whether a Red Notice or Diffusion exists in your name and which country requested it.
  2. Review the Underlying CaseThe foreign charges, procedural history, and supporting documents are reviewed for grounds of challenge.
  3. Prepare the CCF RequestA formal request for access, correction, or deletion is prepared and filed with Interpol's Commission for the Control of Files.
  4. CCF ReviewThe CCF examines whether the notice complies with Interpol's rules and may request information from the requesting country.
  5. Respond to Any Detention or Extradition RequestIf a person is provisionally arrested or a formal extradition request is filed, the case proceeds before the UAE authorities and courts, where defenses can be raised.
  6. OutcomeThe result may be deletion of the notice, a refusal of extradition, or another resolution — after which travel status can be confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Red Notice the same as an international arrest warrant?

No. It is a request for cooperation, not an arrest warrant. Each member country decides independently whether to act on it, although it can lead to detention in practice.

Can a Red Notice be removed?

Yes, in some cases. A request can be made to the CCF, usually arguing that the notice breaches Interpol's rules, for example because it is predominantly political in nature.

Will I be extradited automatically if a Red Notice exists?

No. Extradition is a separate legal process in which the UAE authorities and courts review the requesting country's application and any available defenses.

Can I check whether a Red Notice exists in my name?

Interpol does not publish all notices, and some are not public. A lawyer can advise on how to establish your position, including through a request to the CCF.

How long does a CCF request take?

Timelines vary, but a standard request generally takes several months. An urgent procedure exists in limited circumstances.
