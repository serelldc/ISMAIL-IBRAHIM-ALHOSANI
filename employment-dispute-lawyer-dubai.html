Employment Dispute Lawyer in Dubai

Workplace disputes take many forms beyond termination — disagreements over unpaid wages, disputed performance issues, discrimination complaints, and conflicts over how contract terms should be interpreted all require their own approach.

Employment disputes in the UAE typically move through a structured process, often starting with a complaint to the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation before escalating to labor court if not resolved.

Wage and payment disputes require careful documentation of what was actually owed under the contract versus what was paid, which becomes the basis for any claim pursued through MOHRE or the courts.

Discrimination and workplace conduct complaints require a different evidentiary approach, focused on establishing a pattern of treatment rather than a single missed payment or calculation error.

We represent both employers and employees in workplace disputes, from initial negotiation through MOHRE complaint processes and, where necessary, labor court litigation.

How We Handle Employment Disputes

  1. Dispute AssessmentWe assess the nature of the dispute and the strength of the position based on the applicable contract and labor law provisions.
  2. Documentation GatheringWe gather the contracts, payment records, and correspondence needed to support the claim or defense.
  3. Negotiated Resolution AttemptWhere appropriate, we attempt to resolve the dispute through direct negotiation before pursuing formal proceedings.
  4. MOHRE Complaint FilingWhere negotiation isn't successful, we file or respond to a formal complaint with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation.
  5. Labor Court RepresentationWhere the dispute isn't resolved at the MOHRE stage, we represent the client's position before the labor court.
  6. Resolution and EnforcementWe work toward resolution and ensure any settlement or judgment is properly enforced.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the typical process for resolving an employment dispute in the UAE?

Most disputes start with a complaint to the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, which will attempt to mediate before the matter can be escalated to labor court if unresolved.

What evidence is needed to support a wage dispute claim?

The employment contract, payment records, and any correspondence about compensation are central, and we help gather and organize this documentation to support the claim.

How are discrimination complaints handled differently from wage disputes?

Discrimination complaints typically require establishing a pattern of treatment through witness accounts and communications, rather than the payment calculations central to wage disputes.

What happens if a MOHRE complaint doesn't resolve the dispute?

The matter can generally be escalated to labor court, where we represent the client's position through formal litigation.

Can employment disputes be resolved without going through MOHRE at all?

Yes, where both parties are willing, direct negotiation can resolve a dispute more quickly, and we attempt this route where appropriate before filing a formal complaint.
