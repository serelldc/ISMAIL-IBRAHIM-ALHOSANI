Labor Claim Lawyer in Dubai

Filing a labor claim in the UAE follows a specific procedural path, and understanding what each stage requires — from the initial MOHRE complaint through to court judgment — affects how efficiently a claim actually gets resolved.

A labor claim generally begins with registering a complaint with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, which will attempt to mediate before referring unresolved matters to the labor court for formal adjudication.

The initial complaint needs to accurately set out the claim's basis and supporting facts, since the framing established at this stage carries through if the matter is later referred to court.

Once a matter reaches the labor court, the claim proceeds through the standard litigation process, requiring evidence, potentially witness testimony, and a judgment that then needs to be enforced.

We handle labor claims from initial MOHRE registration through mediation, court referral where necessary, litigation, and enforcement of the resulting judgment.

How We Handle Labor Claims

  1. Claim AssessmentWe assess the merits of the claim and the evidence available to support it before proceeding.
  2. MOHRE Complaint RegistrationWe register the formal complaint with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, framing the claim accurately from the outset.
  3. Mediation ParticipationWe represent the client through MOHRE's mediation process, seeking resolution without the need for court proceedings.
  4. Court Referral and FilingWhere mediation doesn't resolve the matter, we handle the referral and filing of the claim with the labor court.
  5. Litigation RepresentationWe represent the client's position throughout the court proceedings, including evidence submission and hearings.
  6. Judgment EnforcementOnce judgment is obtained, we pursue its enforcement to ensure the awarded amounts are actually recovered.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the first step in filing a labor claim in the UAE?

Most labor claims begin with registering a formal complaint with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, which will attempt mediation before the matter can proceed to court.

What happens during MOHRE mediation?

The ministry facilitates discussion between the parties to try to reach a resolution without formal litigation, and both sides typically present their position for consideration.

What happens if mediation doesn't resolve the labor claim?

The matter is generally referred to the labor court, where the claim proceeds through formal litigation with evidence submission and, where relevant, hearings.

How long does a labor claim typically take to resolve?

Timing varies based on whether the matter resolves at mediation or requires full court proceedings, and we can give a clearer estimate once we understand the specifics of your claim.

What if the other party doesn't comply with a labor court judgment?

We can pursue formal enforcement action to ensure a judgment is actually complied with, which is a distinct process from obtaining the judgment itself.
