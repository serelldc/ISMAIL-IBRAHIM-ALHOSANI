Property Registration & Title Lawyer in Dubai

Ownership of Dubai real estate exists only once it is properly registered — a signed sale contract alone does not transfer legal title, and errors in the registration process can leave a buyer's ownership exposed.

A property registration and title lawyer in Dubai advises buyers, sellers, and owners on registering ownership, mortgages, and interests in real estate with the Dubai Land Department, and resolving registration or title irregularities.

Ownership of real estate in Dubai is confirmed through registration with the Dubai Land Department — for off-plan units, this begins with provisional registration, commonly known as Oqood, under the interim real property register established by Law No. 13 of 2008, converting into a full Title Deed once the project is complete and handed over.

The firm advises on the full registration lifecycle — verifying that an off-plan purchase has been correctly registered to protect the buyer against double-selling, checking a title for existing mortgages or other registered interests before a purchase, and resolving disputes where a registration has been delayed, refused, or appears to contain an error.

A mortgage over Dubai real estate has no legal effect unless registered with the Dubai Land Department under Law No. 14 of 2008, which means confirming a property's registered status is essential before relying on it as security or purchasing it as apparently unencumbered.

How Property Registration Is Handled

  1. Status CheckThe lawyer checks the property's current registration status with the Dubai Land Department, including any registered mortgages or interests.
  2. Document ReviewSale contracts, NOCs, and supporting documents are reviewed for registration readiness.
  3. Registration FilingThe relevant application — Oqood, transfer, or mortgage registration — is filed with the Dubai Land Department.
  4. Fee and NOC CoordinationRegistration fees are calculated and any required developer NOC is obtained.
  5. Trustee Office AttendanceFor resale transfers, the transaction is completed at a DLD-authorized trustee office.
  6. Title Deed ConfirmationThe final registration or Title Deed is confirmed and reviewed for accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Oqood and a Title Deed?

Oqood is the provisional registration recorded during the off-plan construction period, protecting the buyer's interest and preventing the same unit being sold twice; the Title Deed is the final, permanent ownership document issued once the project is complete and the unit is handed over.

Is a mortgage valid if it isn't registered with the Dubai Land Department?

No — under Law No. 14 of 2008, a mortgage over Dubai real estate only has legal effect once it is registered with the Dubai Land Department.

How do I check if a property has a clean title before buying?

A title check through the Dubai Land Department will show the registered owner, any registered mortgages, and other recorded interests, and should be completed before signing a binding purchase agreement.

What if there's an error on my Title Deed?

Errors in a Title Deed can generally be corrected through an application to the Dubai Land Department, though the process and supporting documents required depend on the nature of the error.

Do I need an NOC to sell my property?

Yes — a No Objection Certificate from the developer, confirming that service charges and other dues are settled, is generally required before the Dubai Land Department will process a resale transfer.
