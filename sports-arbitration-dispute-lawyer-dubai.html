Sports Arbitration & Dispute Resolution Lawyer in Dubai

Sports disputes rarely go to ordinary court — most are resolved before a federation's own dispute body, in domestic arbitration, or before the Court of Arbitration for Sport, each with its own procedure and deadlines that can be easy to miss without guidance.

A sports arbitration and dispute resolution lawyer in Dubai represents clubs, athletes, agents, and federations in disputes before domestic arbitral tribunals, federation dispute bodies, and the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) in Lausanne.

Sporting disputes with a UAE dimension can be resolved through several routes: domestic arbitration under Federal Law No. 6 of 2018 on Arbitration (often administered by DIAC), the internal dispute resolution chambers of international federations such as FIFA, or appeal to the Court of Arbitration for Sport, depending on what the governing rules or contract specify.

The firm represents clients at every stage of a sporting dispute — from the initial notice of claim, through submissions and hearings, to enforcement of the final award — in matters spanning contract breach, disciplinary sanctions, transfer disputes, and eligibility challenges.

Because most sporting bodies impose strict, often short, deadlines for filing an appeal or claim, missing a procedural window can forfeit a valid case entirely — early advice on which forum applies and what the applicable time limit is matters as much as the substance of the dispute itself.

How a Sports Arbitration & Dispute Matter Works

  1. Forum & Time Limit AssessmentThe lawyer identifies which dispute body has jurisdiction — federation panel, domestic arbitration, or CAS — and confirms the applicable filing deadline.
  2. Case PreparationEvidence, contractual documentation, and witness statements are gathered and organised to support the claim or defence.
  3. Filing & SubmissionsThe claim, appeal, or defence is filed in accordance with the relevant tribunal's procedural rules, with written submissions prepared and exchanged.
  4. Hearing RepresentationThe client is represented at any hearing before the tribunal, arbitral panel, or CAS, presenting arguments and evidence.
  5. Award or DecisionOnce the tribunal issues its award or decision, the lawyer advises on its effect and any further appeal rights available.
  6. EnforcementWhere the other party does not comply voluntarily, the lawyer pursues recognition and enforcement of the award through the appropriate courts or federation mechanisms.

When You Need a Sports Arbitration & Dispute Resolution Lawyer

  • You are facing a disciplinary charge or sanction from a club or federation and want to appeal it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS)?

CAS, based in Lausanne, Switzerland, is the leading international body for resolving sports-related disputes, including appeals against federation decisions on disciplinary, eligibility, and doping matters.

Do I have to go through my federation's dispute body before going to CAS?

In most cases yes — federation rules typically require that internal remedies be exhausted before an appeal can be brought to CAS, so the correct sequence matters.

How long do I have to file an appeal?

Deadlines vary by federation and forum and are often short, sometimes as little as 21 days from notification of a decision, so early advice on the applicable deadline is essential.

Can UAE domestic arbitration be used for sports disputes?

Yes — disputes not otherwise reserved to a federation's internal process can be arbitrated domestically under Federal Law No. 6 of 2018 on Arbitration, often administered through DIAC.

How is an arbitral award enforced if the other party does not pay?

Enforcement typically involves seeking recognition of the award before the competent courts, or in some cases relying on federation-level sanctions against a non-compliant party.
