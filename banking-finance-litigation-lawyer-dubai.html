Banking & Finance Litigation Lawyer in Dubai

When a banking or financial dispute reaches the courts, the outcome usually turns on documentation, procedure, and strict filing deadlines under UAE civil and commercial law, not on argument alone.

A banking and finance litigation lawyer in Dubai represents banks, corporates, and individuals in contested court proceedings arising from credit facilities, banking documentation, and payment disputes, from the initial claim through judgment and enforcement.

Banking disputes in Dubai are heard before the Dubai Courts or, where the underlying agreement falls within DIFC jurisdiction, the DIFC Courts. Cases frequently turn on the precise wording of facility letters, security documents, and account records, and often involve sums that make early strategic decisions — whether to negotiate, litigate, or pursue precautionary measures — commercially significant.

The firm acts for both sides of banking litigation: representing banks and financial institutions pursuing recovery, and representing borrowers, guarantors, and corporates defending claims or challenging disputed charges, account debits, and enforcement action.

Because UAE banking litigation is largely decided on documentary evidence, often supported by a court-appointed financial expert, the strength of a case depends heavily on how the underlying facility, security, and account documentation is marshalled and presented from the outset.

How Banking & Finance Litigation Works

  1. Case Assessment & Document ReviewThe client's facility agreements, security documents, correspondence, and account statements are reviewed to assess claims, defenses, and litigation risk.
  2. Pre-Action DemandA formal demand or notice is typically issued before filing, setting out the claim or dispute and giving the counterparty an opportunity to respond.
  3. Filing Before the Competent CourtA civil or commercial claim is filed with the Dubai Courts or DIFC Courts, depending on the jurisdiction clause in the underlying banking documentation.
  4. Pleadings & Case ManagementThe parties exchange statements of claim and defense, supporting documents, and any procedural applications, under the court's case management timetable.
  5. Expert ExaminationIn most banking cases the court appoints a financial expert to review account records and calculations; the expert's report can be challenged before judgment.
  6. Judgment & EnforcementThe court issues its judgment, and, where favorable, enforcement follows against the debtor's assets, bank accounts, or other recoverable property.

When You Need a Banking & Finance Litigation Lawyer

  • You have received a claim or demand from a bank over an unpaid credit facility.
  • You need to recover unpaid amounts under a loan, overdraft, or facility agreement from a defaulting borrower.
  • You are disputing account debits, interest or profit charges, or fees you believe were wrongly applied.
  • A cheque you issued or received has bounced and civil or criminal proceedings have followed.
  • Your bank account has been frozen, or funds attached, under a precautionary or execution order.
  • You need to challenge, or defend, a garnishment order or asset freeze connected to a banking dispute.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which court hears banking disputes in Dubai?

It depends on the jurisdiction clause in the banking documentation and the parties involved — most banking disputes go to the Dubai Courts, but agreements with DIFC-regulated entities may fall under DIFC Courts jurisdiction.

What happens if I don't respond to a bank's claim?

The court can proceed in your absence and issue a judgment against you, so it's important to respond and file a defense within the applicable deadlines.

Can a bank freeze my account without a court order?

Banks generally need a legal basis to restrict an account, and a full freeze of assets typically follows a court-ordered precautionary attachment rather than unilateral bank action.

How long does banking litigation take in Dubai?

Timelines vary with case complexity and whether a financial expert is appointed, but many first-instance banking disputes are resolved within a year, with the possibility of appeal extending the timeline further.

Can a foreign judgment be enforced against assets in the UAE?

Generally yes, subject to a recognition and enforcement procedure before the UAE courts, which depends on treaties or reciprocity arrangements with the judgment's country of origin.
