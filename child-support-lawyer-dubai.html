Child Support Lawyer in Dubai

Child support in the UAE is treated as a distinct obligation from spousal maintenance — a duty owed to the child that generally continues regardless of the parents' personal dispute — and getting the calculation right matters for both the paying and receiving parent.

Child support obligations under UAE personal status law are assessed based on the child's actual needs — including education, healthcare, and living expenses — and the paying parent's financial capacity, rather than a fixed formula applied uniformly to every case.

Support calculations frequently become contested where a paying parent's income isn't transparent or where the child's needs — such as private school fees or medical requirements — are disputed between the parents.

Support obligations don't remain static — a child's needs change as they grow, and a parent's financial capacity can change too, both of which may justify revisiting an existing support order over time.

We represent parents in establishing appropriate child support arrangements, contesting unreasonable claims, and pursuing enforcement where a paying parent isn't meeting their obligations.

How We Handle Child Support Matters

  1. Needs and Financial AssessmentWe assess the child's actual needs and gather financial information on the paying parent to establish an appropriate basis for support.
  2. Calculation and DocumentationWe prepare detailed documentation of needs and expenses to support the support claim or to respond to one made against our client.
  3. NegotiationWe pursue negotiated support arrangements where possible, aiming for terms both parents can sustain.
  4. Court Filing and RepresentationWhere negotiation isn't reached, we file and present the support claim or defense before the Personal Status Court.
  5. Income VerificationWhere a paying parent's income isn't transparent, we pursue financial disclosure to establish an accurate basis for the support obligation.
  6. Modification and EnforcementWe assist with modifying support orders as needs and circumstances change, and with enforcing orders that aren't being complied with.

When to Bring in a Child Support Lawyer

  • You need to establish a child support arrangement as part of or separate from a custody matter.
  • You're facing a child support claim and want to understand what's a reasonable and appropriate amount.
  • Your child's needs have changed — a new school, medical needs, or other significant expenses — and the existing support order needs updating.
  • The other parent's income isn't transparent and you suspect the support amount doesn't reflect their true capacity.
  • A support order isn't being complied with and you need to pursue enforcement.
  • Your financial circumstances have changed significantly, affecting your ability to meet an existing support obligation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the amount of child support determined?

The amount is generally assessed based on the child's actual needs, including education, healthcare, and living expenses, weighed against the paying parent's financial capacity.

Does child support continue regardless of the parents' relationship?

Yes, child support is treated as a distinct obligation owed to the child and generally continues regardless of disputes between the parents.

Can child support be increased if my child's needs change?

Yes, support orders can generally be modified where there's a material change in the child's needs or the paying parent's financial capacity.

What happens if the paying parent stops paying child support?

Non-payment can be pursued through enforcement proceedings, and we assist clients in taking the appropriate legal steps to secure compliance.

Does child support cover private school fees and medical expenses?

Support calculations can account for specific significant expenses like private education or medical needs, though this depends on the circumstances and how the claim is presented.
