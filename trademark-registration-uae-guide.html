How to Register a Trademark in the UAE: Step-by-Step Guide

General information · Not legal advice

Trademark rights in the UAE arise from registration, not use — here is the process from clearance search through to your registration certificate.

The UAE operates a registration-based trademark system, meaning legal rights generally arise from registering a mark with the Ministry of Economy rather than from using it in commerce. Since the UAE joined the Madrid Protocol in December 2021, brand owners can also extend protection to over 125 other member countries through a single international application.

Trademark applications are classified under the Nice Classification system, which groups goods and services into 45 international classes. Choosing the right classes — and enough of them — determines how much of the business the registered mark actually protects.

Once filed, an application goes through examination, and if approved, is published in the Trademark Journal for a 30-day opposition period during which third parties can object. If no opposition is raised, the Ministry issues a registration certificate valid for ten years.

Steps to Register a Trademark in the UAE

  1. Clearance SearchA search of the Ministry's trademark database checks whether a proposed mark conflicts with existing registrations or pending applications in the same class — skipping this step is the most common reason applications fail at examination.
  2. Class SelectionThe mark is classified under the Nice Classification system across the goods and services classes relevant to the business, which determines the scope of protection.
  3. Application FilingThe application, including a clear representation of the mark and required documents, is filed with the Ministry of Economy.
  4. ExaminationThe Ministry reviews the application for compliance with formal requirements and potential conflicts with existing marks.
  5. Publication and Opposition PeriodIf approved, the mark is published in the Trademark Journal for 30 days, during which third parties may file an opposition.
  6. Registration CertificateIf no opposition succeeds, the Ministry issues a certificate confirming registration, valid for ten years from the filing date and renewable indefinitely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does UAE trademark registration take?

If no objections or oppositions are raised, registration typically takes several months from filing to certificate, though timelines vary by class and workload at the Ministry.

How long is a UAE trademark valid?

A registered trademark is valid for ten years from the filing date and can be renewed for further ten-year periods, provided the renewal is filed within the last year of the term or within a three-month grace period after expiry.

Can I register one trademark to cover all my products?

A single application can cover multiple classes, but each class is examined and, in most cases, incurs separate fees — the right class strategy depends on the scope of the business.

What happens if someone opposes my trademark application?

The opposing party must file within the 30-day publication window, after which both sides may submit arguments before the Ministry decides whether registration proceeds.

Can I use the Madrid Protocol to protect my UAE trademark abroad?

Yes — since the UAE joined the Madrid Protocol in December 2021, a UAE-registered mark can serve as the basis for a single international application extending protection to over 125 member countries.
