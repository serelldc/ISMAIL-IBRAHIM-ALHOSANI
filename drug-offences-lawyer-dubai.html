Drug Offences Lawyer in Dubai

UAE drug law applies with a strictness that surprises many visitors and residents — trace amounts, medication legal elsewhere, and even substances detected in a bloodstream without any drugs found on a person can all form the basis of a prosecution. The margin for error is genuinely narrow.

The distinction between possession, consumption, and trafficking carries dramatically different consequences under UAE law, and how an allegation is initially characterized — often based on quantity and circumstances — significantly shapes the entire case from the outset.

Drug offence cases in the UAE frequently involve individuals who did not intend to break the law — a prescription medication legal in their home country, residual trace amounts from prior legal use abroad, or an item carried unknowingly. Establishing the factual circumstances accurately and early is critical, since these details materially affect how the case is characterized and defended.

Testing methodology and chain of custody for seized substances or biological samples are frequently central to a drug offence defense — procedural irregularities in how evidence was collected, stored, or tested can be significant grounds for challenge, particularly in trace-amount cases.

We defend individuals facing drug possession, consumption, and trafficking allegations, including UAE residents, visitors, and travellers transiting through UAE airports, working through both the factual circumstances and the technical evidence underlying the case.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I be prosecuted for a prescription medication that's legal in my home country?

Yes, potentially — the UAE's list of controlled substances differs from many other countries, and medications legal elsewhere can still result in a case if not properly documented or declared.

What happens if drugs are found in my system but not on my person?

UAE law can treat trace evidence of consumption, detected through testing, as a basis for a case even without any substance being physically found, which is why understanding the specific evidence against you matters.

What's the difference between a possession and a trafficking charge?

Trafficking allegations generally involve an inference of intent to distribute, often based on quantity or other circumstantial factors, and carry substantially more severe consequences than simple possession.

Can I be held at the airport if I'm just transiting through Dubai?

Yes — being in transit doesn't exempt a traveller from UAE drug laws, and detentions of transiting passengers over suspected substances do occur, requiring urgent representation.

Does UAE law distinguish between different types of controlled substances?

Yes, the legal treatment and consequences can vary depending on the specific substance and quantity involved, which is why an accurate factual and legal assessment early in the case matters significantly.
