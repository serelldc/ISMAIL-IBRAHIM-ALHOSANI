Travel Ban Lawyer in Dubai

A travel ban can be imposed for reasons ranging from an active criminal investigation to an unresolved civil debt, and discovering one at the airport — often without prior notice — leaves little room to react without immediate legal guidance.

Travel bans in the UAE can originate from the Public Prosecution in connection with a criminal case, from a civil court in connection with an unpaid debt or judgment, or from other administrative sources, and the correct procedure for lifting each type differs significantly depending on its origin.

A travel ban tied to a criminal investigation typically requires addressing the underlying case — through resolution, bail, or a formal application to the Public Prosecution or court demonstrating the ban is no longer necessary or proportionate. Understanding which authority actually imposed the ban is the first and most important step.

A travel ban linked to a civil debt or judgment is a different mechanism entirely, often used by creditors to secure payment or compel a debtor's presence in the jurisdiction, and can potentially be lifted through settlement, payment arrangement, or a court application addressing the underlying obligation.

We assist clients in identifying the source and basis of a travel ban, and in pursuing the appropriate legal channel to have it lifted or challenged, whether that requires engaging with the Public Prosecution, a civil court, or a settlement with the party who requested it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find out why a travel ban was placed on me?

The source can typically be identified through the relevant authority — the Public Prosecution for criminal matters or the civil court system for debt-related bans — and we assist clients in making this determination promptly.

Can a travel ban linked to a debt be lifted by paying the amount owed?

In many cases, settling or arranging payment of the underlying debt is the most direct route to having a civil travel ban lifted, though the specific procedure depends on the case.

How long does it take to lift a travel ban?

Timelines vary significantly depending on the ban's source, the complexity of the underlying matter, and the responsiveness of the relevant authority, so early action improves the likelihood of a faster resolution.

Can a travel ban be imposed without prior notice?

Yes, individuals sometimes discover a travel ban only when attempting to leave the country, which is why understanding your legal exposure in an ongoing dispute or investigation in advance is valuable.

Is a criminal travel ban handled differently from a civil one?

Yes, they involve different authorities and different procedures — a criminal ban typically requires engaging with the Public Prosecution or court handling the case, while a civil ban usually involves the creditor and civil court system.
