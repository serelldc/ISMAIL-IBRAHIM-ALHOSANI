Employment Contract Lawyer in Dubai

An employment contract sets the terms that govern the entire working relationship, and gaps or non-compliant clauses discovered only after a dispute arises are far more costly to fix than getting the drafting right from the outset.

UAE employment contracts must align with the labor law's mandatory provisions and MOHRE requirements — terms that conflict with statutory minimums are generally unenforceable regardless of what the contract states.

Non-compete and confidentiality clauses require careful drafting to be enforceable, since overly broad restrictions on an employee's future work are frequently struck down or narrowed by UAE courts.

Probationary periods, notice requirements, and termination provisions each have statutory boundaries that a contract can operate within but generally cannot override to the employee's detriment.

We draft and review employment contracts for both employers and senior employees, ensuring terms are enforceable, compliant with mandatory labor law provisions, and clearly reflect the actual working arrangement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an employment contract include terms below the statutory minimum protections?

Generally no — provisions that conflict with mandatory labor law minimums are typically unenforceable even if the employee signed the contract accepting them.

Are non-compete clauses enforceable in the UAE?

They can be, but only within reasonable limits on duration, geography, and scope, and overly broad restrictions are frequently narrowed or struck down by courts.

What should I check before signing an employment contract?

Key areas include compensation structure, notice and termination terms, non-compete or confidentiality obligations, and whether the terms match what was verbally discussed.

Do employment contract terms need to match what's registered with MOHRE?

Yes, discrepancies between the actual contract terms and what's registered can create complications, which is why alignment between the two is important.

Can an existing employment contract be amended?

Yes, but amendments should be properly documented and, where relevant, reflected in MOHRE registration to avoid disputes about which terms actually govern the relationship.
