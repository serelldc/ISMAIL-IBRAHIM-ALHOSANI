Labor & Employment Law

Employment contracts, termination disputes, and labor claims.

Labor and Employment Lawyer in Dubai

Our labor and employment team advises employees and employers in Dubai on employment contracts, wrongful termination, unpaid wages, end-of-service gratuity, and labor claims. Most private-sector employment relationships in the UAE are governed by Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on the Regulation of Labor Relations, and many disputes begin with a complaint to the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) before reaching the courts.

We review your contract, payslips, and correspondence first, explain what you can claim or what exposure you face, and recommend the most practical route, whether that is negotiation, a MOHRE complaint, or court proceedings. Labor and Employment FAQs

What should I do if I was terminated without notice in Dubai?

Keep copies of your employment contract, payslips, termination letter, and any messages with your employer, then get legal advice before signing anything. A lawyer can assess whether the termination was lawful and what notice, compensation, or end-of-service dues you may be entitled to.

How is end-of-service gratuity calculated?

Gratuity generally depends on your length of service and your last basic salary, as set out in UAE labor law. A lawyer can calculate what you are owed based on your contract and employment dates.

Where do I file a labor complaint in the UAE?

Labor disputes usually begin with a complaint to MOHRE, which attempts to settle the matter. If it is not resolved, the dispute can be referred to the competent labor court. A lawyer can prepare the complaint and represent you at each stage.

Do you represent employers as well as employees?

Yes. We advise employers on employment contracts, internal policies, terminations, and responding to labor claims, as well as representing employees.
