Infrastructure & Mega Project Lawyer in Dubai

Infrastructure and mega projects rarely fail because of one bad clause — they fail because dozens of contracts, joint venture agreements, and government approvals have to move together, and a gap between any two of them becomes expensive fast. Advising on these projects means seeing the whole structure, not just the document in front of you.

Public-private partnerships, joint ventures between multiple contractors, and multi-tier subcontracting chains each introduce their own layer of risk allocation — and on a mega project, misalignment between these layers is usually where disputes originate.

Large infrastructure works in Dubai and across the UAE — transport, utilities, energy, and mixed-use mega developments — typically involve a web of interlinked agreements: the main contract, a joint venture or consortium agreement between the contracting parties, back-to-back subcontracts, and often a concession or PPP framework with a government or semi-government counterparty. Each layer needs to be consistent with the others.

Joint venture and consortium structures bring their own disputes, separate from the underlying construction issues — profit-sharing disagreements, differing views on how to respond to an employer claim, or one JV partner wanting to settle while another wants to litigate. These internal disputes can be as commercially significant as the external ones.

We advise developers, government entities, contractors, and JV/consortium members on the legal structuring, contract administration, and dispute resolution of large-scale infrastructure and mega projects in the UAE.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes mega project disputes different from standard construction disputes?

The main difference is scale and structure — a mega project dispute often involves multiple contracting parties, several layers of subcontracts, and sometimes internal disagreements between JV partners, all of which need to be managed alongside the underlying claim itself.

Can I be liable for a JV partner's actions on a project?

It depends on how the joint venture or consortium agreement and the main contract are structured — many are jointly and severally liable to the employer, meaning liability exposure should be reviewed carefully before entering the structure, not after a dispute arises.

Do PPP projects follow different dispute resolution rules?

Often yes — PPP and concession agreements frequently contain their own dispute escalation mechanisms, sometimes involving government-specific procedures, which need to be identified and followed alongside any FIDIC-based construction contract sitting underneath the concession.

What happens if my JV partner wants to settle a claim and I don't agree?

This depends on the decision-making provisions in your joint venture agreement — some require unanimous consent for settlement decisions, others allow majority decisions, so the governing document needs to be reviewed to determine your rights.

How early should legal advice be involved in a mega project?

Ideally at the structuring stage, before the JV agreement, main contract, and subcontracts are finalised — misalignments between these documents are far cheaper to fix before signature than after a dispute has crystallised.
