Theft & Assault Lawyer in Dubai

Theft and assault allegations often arise in the heat of a moment — a workplace dispute, a family conflict, a physical altercation — and the details that get established in the first police report frequently shape the case far more than what's argued later at trial.

Assault charges in the UAE vary significantly in severity depending on the injury caused, the circumstances, and any aggravating factors, while theft allegations depend heavily on establishing intent to permanently deprive someone of property rather than a dispute over ownership or a misunderstanding.

Physical altercations frequently involve conflicting accounts from both sides, and how the initial police report characterizes the incident — who is treated as the aggressor, what injuries are documented, what witnesses are recorded — significantly shapes how the case proceeds from that point. Medical reports and witness statements gathered early are often decisive.

Theft allegations can arise from situations that are genuinely disputes over ownership or authorization rather than clear-cut criminal conduct — a family disagreement over property, a workplace dispute over company equipment, or confusion about permission to use something. Establishing the accused's actual intent is central to both prosecuting and defending these cases.

We represent both complainants and defendants in theft and assault matters, working through the factual circumstances, medical and witness evidence, and the specific intent element each allegation requires, from the initial police stage through trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

What has to be proven for a theft allegation to succeed?

Generally, the prosecution needs to establish that property was taken without consent and with the intent to permanently deprive the owner of it — a genuine dispute over ownership or permission can undermine that intent element.

Does the severity of an assault charge depend on the injury caused?

Yes, injury severity is a significant factor in how an assault is classified and the consequences that follow, along with circumstances such as premeditation or use of a weapon.

Can a theft or assault matter be resolved through compensation to the victim?

In some cases, compensation or reconciliation between the parties can influence how a matter proceeds, though this depends heavily on the severity of the offence and the stage of proceedings.

What should I do immediately after being involved in a physical altercation?

Seek medical attention promptly if injured, document what happened as thoroughly as possible, and obtain legal advice before giving a detailed statement to police if you're uncertain how the incident will be characterized.

Can I be charged with theft over a family property dispute?

It's possible if the other party frames the matter as theft rather than a civil ownership dispute, which is why an early, well-prepared response addressing the actual nature of the disagreement matters.
