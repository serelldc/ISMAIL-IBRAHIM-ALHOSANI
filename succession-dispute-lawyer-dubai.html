Succession Dispute Lawyer in Dubai

Disagreements among heirs over a will's validity, the size of a claimed share, or an executor's conduct can escalate into formal litigation — and having representation focused specifically on succession disputes matters at that stage.

Succession disputes take several distinct forms: challenges to a will's validity on grounds such as capacity or undue influence, disagreements over the size of a claimed share, and complaints about how an executor or administrator has managed the estate.

A will validity challenge requires establishing specific legal grounds — the testator lacked capacity, was subject to undue influence, or the document itself has procedural defects — rather than simply disagreeing with its outcome.

Disputes over the size of a share often arise from differing interpretations of a will's language or from disagreement about whether certain assets should have been included in the estate calculation at all.

We represent heirs on both sides of succession disputes — those challenging a will or distribution and those defending against a challenge — through negotiation where possible and litigation where necessary.

When to Bring in a Succession Dispute Lawyer

Frequently Asked Questions

On what grounds can a will be challenged?

Common grounds include the testator lacking mental capacity when the will was made, undue influence by another party, and procedural defects in how the will was executed.

What if I simply disagree with what a will says, without a specific legal defect?

A will generally can't be challenged just because an heir is unhappy with the outcome — a specific legal ground such as incapacity, undue influence, or a procedural defect is typically required.

Can disputes between heirs be resolved without going to court?

Often yes — we pursue negotiated resolution between heirs where possible, reserving formal proceedings for situations where negotiation doesn't succeed.

What can be done if an executor isn't managing the estate properly?

Heirs generally have the ability to raise formal complaints about an executor's conduct, and depending on the severity, this can include seeking their removal or accounting for their actions.

How long do succession disputes typically take to resolve?

This varies significantly depending on the complexity of the dispute and whether it's resolved through negotiation or requires full court proceedings, and we can provide a clearer estimate after reviewing your situation.
