Construction Payment & Variation Lawyer in Dubai

Payment disputes are the most common source of construction conflict — a certificate is undervalued, a variation isn't priced, or payment is withheld beyond what the contract allows. Resolving them usually turns on the payment mechanism buried in the contract's small print.

Most FIDIC-based contracts set out a defined payment cycle — application, certification, and payment within specific timeframes — with interest and, in some cases, a right to suspend work if payment is wrongfully withheld. Knowing exactly where in that cycle a dispute sits determines what remedy is actually available.

Payment disputes typically arise at one of a few predictable points: the engineer undervalues an interim application, a variation is instructed but never formally priced or agreed, or a certified sum simply isn't paid on time. Each has a different contractual remedy, and pursuing the wrong one wastes time the underlying claim doesn't always have.

Variation claims carry their own procedural traps — many contracts require a variation to be instructed in writing, priced according to a specific mechanism, and notified within a set timeframe before it becomes a valid claim. Verbal instructions or informal site agreements are common in practice but weak in a dispute if the contractual variation procedure wasn't followed.

We act for contractors pursuing unpaid certificates and unpriced variations, and for employers disputing valuations or defending against inflated variation claims, working through the payment mechanism clause by clause to identify what's actually owed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I suspend work if I'm not paid?

Many FIDIC-based and standard construction contracts include a suspension right for non-payment beyond a specified period, but the notice requirements must be followed precisely — suspending without proper notice can itself put you in breach.

What if a variation was instructed verbally on site?

It depends on the contract's variation procedure. Some contracts require written instruction to create a valid variation claim, while others allow subsequent written confirmation of a verbal instruction — the specific wording determines whether the claim holds up.

How is a disputed payment certificate challenged?

Typically through a formal notice of dispute referencing the contractual valuation mechanism, supported by measured records and pricing evidence showing why the certified amount is incorrect.

Is interest payable on late payment?

Most standard construction contracts include a contractual interest provision for late payment, and UAE law also provides for statutory interest in certain circumstances — both should be factored into the amount claimed.

What happens to unresolved payment disputes at the end of a project?

They typically get rolled into the final account negotiation, which is why unresolved interim disputes should still be formally recorded and preserved even if not actively pursued at the time — otherwise they risk being overlooked or waived.
