Trademark Lawyer in Dubai

Securing a trademark in the UAE starts well before filing — a clearance search to confirm the mark is available, followed by a registration strategy that covers the right classes for how the business actually operates.

Trademark protection in the UAE is registration-based, meaning rights generally arise from registering the mark with the Ministry of Economy rather than from use alone, which makes early and accurate filing important.

A clearance search checks whether a proposed mark conflicts with existing registrations or pending applications, helping avoid a costly rebrand after significant investment in a name or logo.

Class selection determines the scope of protection a registered trademark actually provides, and businesses that operate across multiple product or service lines often need multi-class filing strategies.

We handle trademark matters from initial clearance searching through registration and the ongoing management of a growing trademark portfolio, including renewals and monitoring.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a clearance search necessary before filing?

A clearance search identifies existing registrations or pending applications that could conflict with a proposed mark, helping avoid rejection or a costly rebrand after investment has already been made.

How does the UAE's registration-based system affect my rights?

Because rights generally arise from registration rather than use alone, filing early is important — using a mark without registering it typically provides more limited protection.

What happens if the trademark office objects to my application?

We review the grounds for objection and prepare a formal response addressing the specific concerns raised, which is often necessary to move the application toward registration.

How many classes should I file my trademark under?

This depends on the full scope of goods or services the business offers or plans to offer, and we help identify the classes that provide appropriate coverage without unnecessary cost.

Does a trademark registration need to be renewed?

Yes, trademark registrations require periodic renewal to remain in force, and we help track and manage these deadlines across a portfolio.
