Real Estate Due Diligence Lawyer in Dubai

The problems that surface after a Dubai property purchase — an undisclosed mortgage, unpaid service charges, a stalled off-plan project — are almost always things that proper due diligence would have caught beforehand.

A real estate due diligence lawyer in Dubai investigates a property's legal and financial standing before purchase, covering title, registered mortgages, service charge arrears, and developer track record.

Real estate due diligence in Dubai is not a single statutory checklist but a body of practice built around what the Dubai Land Department's registration system, the jointly owned property framework under Law No. 6 of 2019, and a developer's own compliance history can reveal about a property before a buyer commits to it.

The firm conducts due diligence covering title verification and registered ownership, any mortgage or other charge registered against the property, outstanding service charges that can block a resale NOC, and, for off-plan purchases, the project's escrow account standing and construction progress against its RERA registration.

Due diligence findings shape more than just the decision to proceed — they often become the basis for renegotiating price, requiring the seller to clear specific liabilities before completion, or building protective conditions into the sale agreement itself.

How Due Diligence Is Conducted

  1. Instruction and ScopeThe lawyer confirms the scope of due diligence required based on the type and value of the transaction.
  2. Title and Registration CheckThe property's registered ownership and any mortgages or interests are verified with the Dubai Land Department.
  3. Service Charge and Arrears CheckOutstanding service charges and community fees are checked, particularly for jointly owned properties.
  4. Developer and Project CheckFor off-plan purchases, the project's RERA registration, escrow status, and construction progress are reviewed.
  5. Findings ReportA clear report is prepared setting out the findings and any recommended actions before completion.
  6. Completion ConditionsWhere issues are found, protective conditions or price adjustments are built into the sale agreement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does real estate due diligence in Dubai actually cover?

At minimum, it covers verifying the registered owner and title, checking for mortgages or other registered charges, and confirming service charges are up to date; for off-plan purchases it extends to the project's escrow and RERA registration status.

Can service charge arrears block my purchase?

Yes — unpaid service charges under the jointly owned property framework can prevent the seller from obtaining the developer's NOC, which is generally required before the Dubai Land Department will process a transfer.

How do I check if an off-plan project is legitimate?

Verifying the project's RERA registration and confirming that buyer payments are held in a compliant escrow account under Law No. 8 of 2007 are the key checks before committing to an off-plan purchase.

Is due diligence necessary for a cash purchase with no mortgage?

Yes — due diligence protects against risks unrelated to financing, such as an undisclosed existing mortgage on the seller's side, unpaid service charges, or title irregularities, all of which can affect a cash buyer just as much.

How long does due diligence typically take?

Straightforward resale due diligence can often be completed within days once the Dubai Land Department and building management respond to requests, while off-plan or complex corporate-structure purchases can take longer depending on how quickly the necessary confirmations are obtained.
