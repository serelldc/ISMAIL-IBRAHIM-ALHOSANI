Estate Distribution Lawyer in Dubai

Once a will has been admitted or a succession order issued, the practical work of allocating and transferring specific assets to each heir begins — and that process has its own procedural and calculation challenges.

Distribution involves translating a court order or will into actual transfers: retitling property, closing or transferring bank accounts, allocating shares in jointly held assets, and calculating each heir's precise entitlement where percentages or fractional shares are involved.

Where an estate includes assets that can't simply be divided — a single property, a business, a vehicle — distribution requires either an agreed valuation and buyout arrangement among heirs or a structured sale with proceeds divided according to entitlement.

Multiple heirs receiving fractional shares of the same asset class often need a clear, calculated breakdown to avoid later disagreement about what each party is actually owed.

We manage the distribution phase from the point a will or succession order is finalized through to completed asset transfers, coordinating with relevant registries and institutions to execute the reallocation.

How We Handle Estate Distribution

  1. Reviewing the Will or Succession OrderWe start from the finalized will or succession order to establish exactly what each heir is entitled to receive.
  2. Asset Valuation Where NeededFor assets that can't be divided directly, we coordinate valuation to support buyout or sale arrangements among heirs.
  3. Share CalculationWe calculate each heir's precise entitlement, particularly where percentages or fractional interests in the same asset are involved.
  4. Property and Title TransferWe manage the retitling process for real estate and other registrable assets to reflect the new ownership.
  5. Bank Account and Financial Asset TransferWe coordinate with financial institutions to transfer or close accounts and distribute financial assets according to entitlement.
  6. Final Distribution ConfirmationWe confirm each heir has received their allocated share and that all transfers have been properly completed and documented.

When to Bring in an Estate Distribution Lawyer

  • A will has been admitted or a succession order issued and you need help executing the actual asset transfers.
  • The estate includes an asset, like a single property or business, that can't simply be split among multiple heirs.

  • Heirs need to agree on a valuation or buyout arrangement for an asset none of them wants to sell.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between probate and distribution?

Probate or a succession order establishes legal entitlement to the estate, while distribution is the subsequent process of actually transferring specific assets to each heir.

What happens if an asset can't be physically divided among heirs?

Typically either the heirs agree on a valuation and one buys out the others' shares, or the asset is sold and proceeds are divided according to each heir's entitlement.

How are fractional shares in an asset calculated?

Shares are calculated based on the entitlements set out in the will or succession order, which we translate into precise figures to avoid disputes about what each heir is owed.

Can you help transfer property title into an heir's name?

Yes, we manage the retitling process for real estate and other registrable assets to reflect the entitlements established in the will or succession order.

How long does the distribution process typically take?

Timing depends on the complexity of the estate, the number of heirs, and whether any assets require valuation or sale, and we can give a more specific estimate once we review your situation.
