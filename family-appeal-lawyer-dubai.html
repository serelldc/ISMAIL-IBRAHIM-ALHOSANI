Family Appeal Lawyer in Dubai

Family court judgments — on divorce, custody, or maintenance — can be appealed, but strict deadlines apply, and an appeal succeeds only where a genuine legal or factual error in the original judgment can be shown, not simply because one party disagrees with the outcome.

Appeals of Personal Status Court judgments proceed through defined stages, with the Court of Appeal generally able to review both facts and law, while further appeal to the Court of Cassation is typically limited to legal error rather than a fresh review of the facts.

The appeal window in family matters is short, and missing the deadline generally forecloses the right to challenge the judgment, making prompt engagement with an appeal lawyer important as soon as an unfavorable judgment is received.

Successful appeals typically identify a specific error — whether in how the law was applied, how evidence was weighed, or a procedural irregularity — rather than simply re-arguing the case as it was presented at first instance.

We represent clients appealing unfavorable family court judgments and those defending judgments against appeal, working within the applicable deadlines and building the specific legal arguments the appellate courts require.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to appeal a family court judgment?

Appeal deadlines in family matters are generally short and strictly enforced, so it's important to seek advice as soon as possible after receiving an unfavorable judgment.

Can I appeal simply because I disagree with the outcome?

No, a successful appeal generally requires identifying a specific legal or factual error in the original judgment, not merely disagreement with the result.

What's the difference between the Court of Appeal and the Court of Cassation?

The Court of Appeal can generally review both the facts and the law of a case, while the Court of Cassation typically reviews questions of law only, without re-examining the underlying facts.

Does filing an appeal pause enforcement of the original judgment?

Whether enforcement is paused during an appeal depends on the type of judgment and the specific procedural rules that apply, so this should be assessed early in the appeal process.

What are my chances of success on appeal?

Prospects depend heavily on whether a genuine legal or factual error can be identified in the original judgment, and we provide a realistic assessment after reviewing the judgment and case file.
