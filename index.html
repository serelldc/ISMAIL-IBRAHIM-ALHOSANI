    About Ismail Ibrahim Alhosani

    Advocate and legal consultant based in Dubai, providing representation and legal advice across the United Arab Emirates.

    Ismail Ibrahim Alhosani Advocates & Legal Consultants advises individuals and businesses on family, criminal, civil, commercial, and real estate matters across the UAE. The firm is registered with the UAE Ministry of Justice and handles cases before local courts as well as DIFC courts.

    Consultations are available in Arabic, English, Chinese, Russian, and German, allowing the firm to serve UAE nationals, expatriates, and international clients directly, without relying on intermediaries.
