Marriage Contracts & Prenuptial Agreements Lawyer in Dubai

A well-drafted marriage contract or prenuptial agreement sets clear expectations before disputes arise, addressing financial matters and other terms in a way that can prevent significant conflict if the marriage later runs into difficulty.

Marriage contracts and prenuptial agreements in the UAE must be structured carefully to be enforceable — provisions that conflict with mandatory principles of UAE personal status law may not be given effect, making careful drafting essential rather than a simple formality.

For expatriate couples in particular, a prenuptial agreement can address which country's law is intended to govern financial matters, how property acquired during the marriage will be treated, and how assets held in multiple jurisdictions will be approached.

Where a marriage contract was executed abroad before relocating to the UAE, understanding how it will be treated locally — and whether any additional registration or documentation is needed — is important well before any dispute arises.

We draft marriage contracts and prenuptial agreements tailored to each couple's circumstances, and advise on the recognition and enforceability of foreign marriage contracts within the UAE legal system.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are prenuptial agreements enforceable under UAE law?

Prenuptial agreements can be enforceable if carefully structured, but provisions that conflict with mandatory principles of UAE personal status law may not be given effect, making proper drafting essential.

Will my foreign marriage contract be recognized in the UAE?

Foreign marriage contracts are generally reviewed and can be given weight, though how they're treated depends on the contract's terms and how it interacts with UAE legal principles.

Can a marriage contract address what happens to property acquired during the marriage?

Yes, addressing how property acquired during the marriage will be treated is a common and important element of a well-drafted marriage contract.

Is it too late to create a marriage contract if we're already married?

No, a postnuptial agreement can generally be drafted and formalized even after marriage, addressing financial matters going forward.

Do both spouses need separate legal advice when drafting a marriage contract?

It's generally advisable for each party to obtain independent advice to ensure the agreement is fair, properly understood, and less vulnerable to later challenge.
