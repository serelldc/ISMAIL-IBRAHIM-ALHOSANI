How to File a Patent in the UAE

General information · Not legal advice

Patent protection in the UAE typically takes two to four years from filing to grant — here is what makes an invention patentable, how the filing process works, and what stays protected for 20 years.

The UAE protects patents under Federal Law No. 11 of 2021 on the Regulation and Protection of Industrial Property Rights, administered by the Ministry of Economy. To be patentable, an invention must be new — not previously disclosed to the public — involve an inventive step, and be capable of industrial application. Foreign applicants must file through a registered local agent.

Certain subject matter is excluded from patent protection, including discoveries, scientific theories, mathematical methods, business methods, computer software as such, plant and animal species, and methods of medical or surgical treatment. Weighing these exclusions early shapes both the filing strategy and how the claims are drafted.

Beyond the UAE's national filing route through the Ministry of Economy, inventors can also file through the GCC Patent Office, which provides protection across all six Gulf Cooperation Council states through a single application — a route worth considering for inventions with a wider regional market.

Steps to File a Patent in the UAE

  1. Application PreparationThe application is prepared with a complete specification, claims defining the scope of protection, an abstract, and the names of the applicant and inventor.
  2. FilingThe application is filed with the Ministry of Economy, with a registered local agent handling filing on behalf of foreign applicants.
  3. Formal ExaminationThe Ministry reviews the application for completeness and compliance with formal filing requirements.
  4. Requesting Substantive ExaminationSubstantive examination is not automatic — the applicant must request it and pay the relevant fees within three years of the filing or priority date.
  5. Substantive Examination and PublicationThe application is examined for novelty, inventive step, and industrial applicability, and published in the official gazette.
  6. Registration and CertificateOnce granted, the Ministry issues a patent certificate, valid for 20 years from the filing date, subject to annual renewal fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a patent granted in the UAE?

The process typically takes two to four years from filing to grant, depending on the technical complexity of the invention and examiner workload, since substantive examination must be separately requested.

How long does UAE patent protection last?

Patent protection lasts 20 years from the filing date, provided annual renewal fees are paid to keep it in force.

What inventions cannot be patented in the UAE?

Discoveries, scientific theories, mathematical methods, business methods, computer software as such, plant and animal species, and methods of medical or surgical treatment are excluded from patent protection.

Should I file nationally in the UAE or through the GCC Patent Office?

It depends on your target market. A UAE national filing protects the invention only in the UAE, while a GCC filing extends protection across all six Gulf Cooperation Council states through a single application.

What happens if I miss the deadline to request substantive examination?

Substantive examination must be requested within three years of the filing or priority date. Missing this deadline can result in the application being treated as withdrawn, so it is worth tracking closely.
