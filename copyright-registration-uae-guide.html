How Copyright Protection Works in the UAE

General information · Not legal advice

Copyright arises automatically the moment a work is created in the UAE — but registration still matters. Here is what's protected, how long it lasts, and when formal registration is worth it.

The UAE protects copyright under Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2021 on Copyright and Neighboring Rights, which took effect in January 2022. Protection arises automatically upon creation of an original work — no registration, publication, or formal notice is required for copyright to exist, and the UAE's membership in the Berne Convention means protected works are also recognized in other member countries.

Protection covers literary, artistic, and scientific works, including books, articles, software, databases, photographs, and audiovisual works. The law distinguishes between economic rights (the right to exploit the work commercially) and moral rights (the right to be credited as the author and to object to distortion of the work), with moral rights remaining with the author even after economic rights are transferred.

Although registration is not required, registering a work with the Ministry of Economy creates official evidence of authorship and ownership, which is often decisive in a dispute and is sometimes required by counterparties in licensing or commercial deals.

How Copyright Registration Works

  1. Prepare the Work and DocumentationA copy or sample of the work is prepared along with a description sufficient to identify it.
  2. Declaration of AuthorshipThe applicant signs a declaration establishing authorship and, where relevant, ownership of the work.
  3. Application FilingThe application, work sample, and declaration are filed with the Ministry of Economy.
  4. Ministry ReviewThe Ministry reviews the application for completeness and compliance with the registration requirements.
  5. Certificate of RegistrationOnce approved, the Ministry issues a certificate that serves as official evidence of the registered ownership.
  6. Use in EnforcementA registered certificate carries significant weight as evidence of ownership if the work is later copied or used without permission.

Signs You Should Register or Enforce Your Copyright

  • You're licensing, selling, or assigning rights to your work and need documented proof of ownership.
  • You've found your work being copied, distributed, or used commercially without permission.
  • A counterparty in a publishing, distribution, or franchising deal requires registered proof of ownership.
  • You've created software, a database, or another work with significant commercial value that you want to protect proactively.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to register my work to have copyright protection?

No. Copyright arises automatically upon creation of an original work. Registration is optional but recommended, since it creates official evidence of ownership that strengthens enforcement.

How long does copyright protection last in the UAE?

For individual authors, protection generally lasts for the author's lifetime plus 50 years. Corporate, anonymous, and audiovisual works are protected for 50 years from publication, while works of applied art and photographs are protected for 25 years from publication.

What types of work are protected?

Literary, artistic, and scientific works are protected, including books, articles, computer software, databases, photographs, and audiovisual works, provided they are original.

What can I do if someone infringes my copyright?

Options include sending a cease-and-desist notice, filing an administrative complaint with the Ministry of Economy, pursuing a civil claim for damages, and, in serious cases, criminal penalties under the law.

Does UAE copyright protection extend internationally?

Yes. As a member of the Berne Convention, the UAE recognizes copyright protection from other member countries, and UAE-protected works are recognized abroad on the same basis.
