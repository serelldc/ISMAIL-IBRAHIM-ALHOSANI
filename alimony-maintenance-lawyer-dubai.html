Alimony & Maintenance Lawyer in Dubai

Spousal maintenance claims in the UAE depend on specific legal criteria — including conduct during the marriage and the reason for its breakdown — that make the outcome far from automatic, and documentation gathered early often determines the result.

Maintenance entitlement under UAE personal status law can be affected by findings about which party is considered responsible for the breakdown of the marriage, making the framing and evidence presented at an early stage significant to the eventual outcome.

Maintenance claims typically address ongoing living expenses during and after divorce proceedings, and the amount awarded depends on factors including the paying spouse's income, the receiving spouse's needs, and the standard of living established during the marriage.

Disputes frequently arise over the appropriate amount of maintenance, particularly where a paying spouse's income isn't straightforward to establish, such as with business owners or those receiving income from multiple sources across jurisdictions.

We represent both spouses seeking maintenance and those defending against claims, working to establish accurate financial pictures and secure outcomes that reflect the applicable legal criteria and the parties' actual circumstances.

How We Handle Alimony & Maintenance Matters

  1. Financial Circumstances AssessmentWe gather and analyze financial information for both spouses to establish an accurate picture of income, assets, and needs.
  2. Entitlement and Amount AnalysisWe assess entitlement to maintenance under the applicable legal framework and the factors likely to affect the amount awarded.

  4. Court Filing and RepresentationWhere negotiation isn't reached, we file and present the maintenance claim or defense before the Personal Status Court.

  6. Modification and EnforcementWe assist with modifying maintenance orders when circumstances change and with enforcing orders that aren't being complied with.

Frequently Asked Questions

What factors affect how much spousal maintenance is awarded?

Factors typically include the paying spouse's income and financial capacity, the receiving spouse's needs, the standard of living during the marriage, and findings about responsibility for the marriage's breakdown.

Does conduct during the marriage affect maintenance entitlement?

Yes, findings about which spouse is considered responsible for the breakdown of the marriage can affect maintenance entitlement under UAE personal status law.

Can spousal maintenance be modified after it's ordered?

Yes, maintenance orders can generally be modified where there's been a material change in either spouse's financial circumstances.

What can I do if my former spouse isn't paying court-ordered maintenance?

Non-payment of a maintenance order can be pursued through enforcement proceedings, and we assist clients in taking the appropriate steps to secure compliance.

How is a self-employed spouse's income assessed for maintenance purposes?

Where income isn't straightforward, such as with business owners, financial disclosure and analysis are often necessary to establish an accurate picture for maintenance purposes.
