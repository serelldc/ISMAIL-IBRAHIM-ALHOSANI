Criminal Law FAQs

What should I do if I am arrested or summoned in Dubai?

Stay calm, be respectful, and ask to contact a lawyer before giving a detailed statement. Do not sign documents you do not understand, and ask for an interpreter if needed. Contact a criminal defense lawyer as early as possible.

Can I be released on bail in the UAE?

Release on bail or on a guarantee may be possible depending on the offence, the evidence, and the prosecution's position. A lawyer can apply for release and propose appropriate conditions.

Can a social media post lead to a criminal case?

Yes. Online posts can in some cases lead to criminal complaints under UAE cybercrime and defamation provisions. If you have received a complaint or summons, seek legal advice before responding.

Do you handle Interpol Red Notice and travel ban cases?

Yes. We advise on challenging Interpol Red Notices, extradition proceedings, and travel bans. See our dedicated Interpol Red Notice and Extradition Lawyer page for details.
