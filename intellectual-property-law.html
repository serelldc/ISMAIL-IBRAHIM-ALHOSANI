Intellectual Property FAQs

Do I need to register my trademark in the UAE?

Registration with the Ministry of Economy gives you exclusive, enforceable rights in the registered classes. Without registration, your ability to stop others from using a similar mark is limited.

How long does IP protection last?

A registered trademark is generally protected for 10 years and can be renewed. Patents are generally protected for 20 years. Copyright arises automatically on creation of an original work.

Can foreign companies register IP in the UAE?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can register trademarks, patents, and designs in the UAE, typically through a local legal representative.

What can I do if someone copies my brand?

Options include a cease-and-desist notice, an administrative complaint, a civil claim for damages, and, in serious cases, criminal proceedings.
