Copyright Lawyer in Dubai

Copyright protection in the UAE arises automatically upon the creation of an original work, but proving ownership, authorship, and the scope of protection often becomes important once a dispute or infringement arises.

Copyright covers a broad range of original works — literary, artistic, musical, and software — and protection is not conditional on registration, though formal deposit can provide useful evidentiary support for ownership claims.

Establishing clear ownership becomes particularly important for commissioned works, employee-created works, and collaborative projects, where the default ownership rules may not match what the parties actually intended.

Software and digital content present distinct copyright questions, including how source code, user interfaces, and databases are protected and how licensing terms should be structured.

We advise on copyright ownership and protection strategy, assist with formal deposit where useful, and represent rights holders in enforcement action against unauthorized use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to register my copyright for it to be protected?

Copyright protection generally arises automatically upon creation of an original work, though formal deposit can provide useful evidence of ownership and creation date if a dispute arises.

Who owns the copyright in work I commissioned from a freelancer?

This depends on the terms of the engagement and applicable default rules, which is why clear contractual provisions on ownership are important when commissioning creative or technical work.

What can be done if someone is using my copyrighted work without permission?

Options range from a formal cease-and-desist notice to litigation, depending on the scope of the infringement and the response received, and we can advise on the most appropriate approach.

Is software protected by copyright in the UAE?

Yes, software generally falls within copyright protection, though specific questions can arise around source code, interfaces, and how licensing terms should be structured.

Who owns copyright in work created by an employee?

This is often addressed by employment contract terms, and we advise businesses on structuring these provisions clearly to avoid later ownership disputes.
