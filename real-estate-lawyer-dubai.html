Real Estate Lawyer in Dubai

Dubai's real estate market runs on a distinct regulatory framework — RERA, the Dubai Land Department, the Rental Disputes Center — and matters that look straightforward on the surface often turn on how that framework applies to the specific transaction.

A real estate lawyer in Dubai advises individuals, investors, and businesses across the full range of property matters — purchases and sales, leasing, registration, disputes, and regulatory compliance under Dubai's real estate legislation.

Real estate legal work in Dubai spans several distinct regulatory frameworks depending on what stage a matter has reached: escrow-protected off-plan purchases under Law No. 8 of 2007, jointly owned property and service charge matters under Law No. 6 of 2019, mortgage registration under Law No. 14 of 2008, and tenancy relationships under Law No. 26 of 2007 as amended — a client's matter often touches more than one of these at once.

The firm advises clients across the property lifecycle — from reviewing a purchase agreement before signing, through registration and financing, to resolving disputes that arise after completion — providing continuity that a client would otherwise have to rebuild with a new adviser at each stage.

Because Dubai real estate disputes are frequently decided by specialized bodies — RERA, the Real Estate Disputes Centre, the Rental Disputes Center — rather than the ordinary courts, effective representation depends on understanding which body has jurisdiction over a given matter and how its process actually works in practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas of real estate law does the firm handle?

The firm covers the full range of Dubai real estate matters, including purchases and sales, off-plan transactions, tenancy and landlord-tenant disputes, mortgage and registration matters, and owners association issues.

How do I know if my issue goes to RERA, the Real Estate Disputes Centre, or the courts?

It depends on the nature of the dispute and the parties involved — a lawyer familiar with the jurisdictional boundaries between these bodies can assess this quickly at the outset of a matter.

Do you advise both individuals and companies?

Yes — the firm advises private individuals, investors, and companies on real estate matters across the Emirate.

I'm new to the Dubai property market — do you advise first-time buyers?

Yes — first-time buyers and investors benefit particularly from early advice, since Dubai's registration, escrow, and regulatory framework differs meaningfully from many other jurisdictions.

Can you handle a matter that touches more than one area, like a mortgage and a tenancy issue together?

Yes — real estate matters frequently overlap across these areas, and having one adviser across the full matter avoids the inefficiency of coordinating between separate specialists.
